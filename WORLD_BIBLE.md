WORLD BIBLE & CAMPAIGN LORE

1. Narrative Pillars & Tonal Framework

Tonal Spectrum: Heroic Realism & Melancholic Gray

The setting balances dark, complex realism with genuine heroic idealism, drawing inspiration from Frieren: Beyond Journey's End, Dragon Age: Origins, Elden Ring, Dark Souls, The Lord of the Rings, and Don Quixote.

No "Evil for the Sake of Evil": Factions and empires do not act out of cartoonish malice. Conflicts are driven by competing survival needs, resource scarcity, ancestral grievances, and conflicting visions for the world's future.

Heroism on All Sides: War features noble, tragic, and heroic figures on every side of the front lines. Antagonists are often people fighting fiercely for their own folk, driven by love, duty, or noble desperation.

The Noble Folly: Ideals matter. Characters can make heroic last stands for the common folk, and tragic heroes may tilt at windmills, pursuing lost causes out of sheer conviction.

Fading Wonder & Stagnation: The world is built upon the ruins of a lost golden age. Magic is stagnant, ancient airships are irreplaceable relics, and the world sits at a crossroads where it will either succumb to a slow, unraveling decay or fight its way into a new era.

2. Party & Avatar Architecture

Character Creation & Companion Agency

Single Custom Avatar: The player creates only their own central main character (Avatar). All other party members are pre-authored, recruitable companions with distinct backgrounds, ancestral ties, personal quests, and ideological alignments.

Companion Approval & Conflict System: Companions react dynamically to player choices throughout the narrative. Decisions—such as choosing to support Cordoval or Torvalla, executing prisoners, or making deals with dark aspects—modify companion approval. If a player's choices severely violate a companion's core convictions, that companion can object, leave the party, or even turn hostile in critical moments.

3. Cosmological Framework & Divine Matrix

Primals vs. The 13

The Primals: Primordial, unyielding forces of nature (Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Growth, Shadow, Void) that exist across all planes. They operate purely according to their elemental nature without mortal concepts of good or evil.

Angels & Demons: Neither angels nor demons are inherently divine creations of the 13. Instead, they are the natural offspring of Primals, born at planar intersections where pure elemental forces collide and merge (e.g., Fire + Growth = Fiery Fiends/Demons; Air + Light = Celestial Beings/Angels).

The 13 Deities: The 13 are the most powerful non-primal entities in existence. They do not own or control their respective domains; rather, they are supreme scholars and masters who study, manipulate, and tap into these domains on a divine scale. If Morwenna traveled directly into the Primal Realm of Shadow, powerful Shadow Primals could challenge or overpower her.

Law of the Final Veil: Deities cannot create or destroy a soul. When a mortal dies, their soul remains in the mortal realm briefly before crossing the Final Veil into the true afterlife. Prior to crossing the veil, resurrection magic can restore life. Once a soul crosses the Final Veil, even death gods cannot retrieve, alter, or restore it.

Avatar & Aspect Mechanics: Deities cannot manifest their full cosmic form without causing cataclysmic reality-warping collateral damage. Instead, they operate through mortal worshipers or instantiate Aspects/Avatars—fragmented incarnations with distinct personalities sent to execute specific tasks. The 13 frequently run multiple plans simultaneously through different aspects, which can lead to divine aspects unintentionally aiding or obstructing each other's goals.

The Pantheon of 13

Aethelis (The Cosmic Weaver): Patron of wizards, astrologers, and chronomancers. Appears as an ancient, bald gnomish man with eyes of rotating distant galaxies, turning an hourglass of stardust.

Kaldian (The Sunlit Warden): Militant guardian deity of the dawn, sacrifice, and vigilance. Appears as a battle-scarred halfling man in gouged golden plate armor with a heavy kite shield.

Cankros (The Whispering Blight): Ancient entity of inevitable rot, decay, and rebirth. Appears as a towering, hooded figure whose face is a cavity of luminescent fungi, centipedes, and blooming rot.

Tavrin (The Laughing Vagabond): Trickster patron of gamblers, bards, outcasts, and merchants. Appears as a roguish traveler in a patchwork coat of faded silks and leathers.

Brada (The Iron Architect): Patron of builders, masons, commerce, and engineering. Appears as an impossibly dense female dwarf in a heavy leather apron holding a mason's hammer and an ironbound ledger.

Kraghtor (The Storm-Crowned Tyrant): Brutal deity of hurricane power, subjugation, and raw martial force. Appears as a hulking orc warlord with tempest-cloud skin and blue lightning crackling across his plate armor.

Morwenna (The Veiled Mourner): Gentle, somber guide of souls toward the Final Veil. Appears as a tall elven woman in mourner's silks, holding an iron lantern of pale blue light and a silver needle.

Oneris (The Endless Trance): Enigmatic entity of dreams and the surreal. Manifests as a shifting silhouette of starlight, smoke, and stained glass, wielding dream-silk fighting fans.

Notable Aspect: An aspect of Oneris manifests in the waking world as an "imaginary friend" to a young NPC girl in a major city—a narrative questline where players can investigate the aspect's true intent or intervene.

Vurrok (The Primal Roar): Deity of untamed wilds, predator/prey cycles, and feral emotion. Appears as a massive figure clad in bark and pelts, wearing a beast skull crowned with jagged antlers.

Severin (The Ashen Judge): Uncompromising god of absolute truth, austerity, and self-mastery. Appears as a gaunt, expressionless humanoid with ash-grey skin, polished obsidian eyes, and a white wood bow staff.

Bathyos (The Abyssal Depth): Eldritch intelligence of lightless ocean trenches and forbidden secrets. Appears as a suspended column of hyper-dense black water teeming with bioluminescent eyes, wielding a razor-kelp garrote.

Stryvan (The Ambitious Flame): God of self-made destiny, meritocracy, and ruthless drive. Appears as a flawless human warrior in gilded armor with a crown forged from melted rival weapons.

Corvyna (The Crimson Matriarch): Deity of bloodlines, childbirth sacrifice, and ancestral tethers. Appears as a parchment-skinned matriarch with glowing red veins, weeping tears of blood and wielding an umbilical-harvesting silver sickle.

4. History, World Structure, & Technology

Global Map & Infrastructure

Terrestrial Sphere: A standard terrestrial planet dominated by deep, stormy oceans, towering mountain spines, ancient rainforest basins, and vast desert expanses.

Maritime Lifeblood: Sea trade is essential for continental survival. Ships make frequent offerings to Tavrin (for fair winds) and Bathyos (to survive the horrors of the lightless deeps).

Airship Relics: Rare, floating skyships powered by ancient Golden Age arcana still navigate high-altitude trade corridors. Because the knowledge to build new skyship engines was lost during the Resource Wars, these ships are irreplaceable national artifacts held exclusively by royal navies, mega-cartels, or archmages.

Historical Timeline

The Age of Wonder (Ancient Era): A golden age of magical breakthroughs, planar conduits, arcana-powered cities, and floating skyship fleets.

The Resource Wars & The Slighting: Greed and line over-expansion triggered global war over mana leylines and primal catalysts. During the climax, a rogue deity slew a core Primal Force (The Primal of Void/Chaos), damaging the fabric of existence and causing reality to scab over.

The Stagnant Concord (Present Day): Centuries of recovery led to an era of stable, conservative peace. Technological and magical innovation has stalled; civilizations rebuild upon old foundations rather than inventing new ones.

The Impending Crossroads: The campaign's current era. Dwindling artifact energy, planar instabilities, and political brinkmanship threaten to tear open the ancient planar scab—plunging the world into a dark age or igniting a dangerous renaissance.

5. Grounded Factions & Regional Geopolitics

Primary Theater Nations (Interactive Campaign Focus)

A. The Sovereign Grand Duchy of Cordoval

Culture & Aesthetics: European/Spanish Mediterranean influence. A proud realm of stone aqueducts, grand cathedrals, terraced vineyards, and heavy cavalry.

Patron Deities: Brada (Commerce & Aqueducts) and Kaldian (Vigilance & Shielding).

Motivation: Cordoval controls vital trade river networks but suffers from aging Golden Age infrastructure. They enforce strict tariffs and border security to prevent economic collapse.

Key Figures: Grand Duke Rodric (Pragmatic ruler fighting corruption) and Lady Yvaine (Heroic knight-commander fighting to protect the peasantry from war).

B. The Frost-Marked Concordat of Torvalla

Culture & Aesthetics: Nordic/Alpine influence. A harsh federation of fjord settlements, rune-smiths, alpine keeps, and disciplined shield-walls.

Patron Deities: Kraghtor (Storms & Power) and Severin (Truth & Discipline).

Motivation: Torvalla’s mountain glaciers are expanding, choking off agricultural valleys. They are pushing south into Cordoval's trade routes out of sheer survival to feed their starving people.

Key Figures: High Thane Torstein (Weary leader forced into expansionist war) and Vaelen (Brilliant, fiercely loyal tactical commander fighting for his starving homeland).

C. The Sun-Gilded League of Calatara

Culture & Aesthetics: African/Asian Coastal influence. A cosmopolitan maritime coalition of archipelago ports, jungle trade networks, floating markets, and mercenary guilds.

Patron Deities: Tavrin (The Open Road) and Bathyos (The Abyssal Deeps).

Motivation: Maintain absolute free trade across ocean routes. They play Cordoval and Torvalla against each other, profiting off mercenary supply contracts while defending trade lanes from deep-sea horrors.

Secondary & Distant World Powers (World Context & Lore)

D. The Grand Sultanate of Shahrazar

Culture & Aesthetics: Middle Eastern / Silk-Road desert influence. A sprawling empire of glass towers, oasis bazaars, subterranean aqueducts, and sand-skimmers.

Patron Deities: Stryvan (Meritocracy & Destiny) and Aethelis (Astrology & Time).

Role in World: Master alchemists and keepers of ancient leyline observatories. They export alchemical gunpowder and elemental catalysts to both Cordoval and Torvalla.

E. The Jade Hegemony of Qing-Ling

Culture & Aesthetics: East Asian / Wuxia influence. An isolated, highly bureaucratic mountain empire beyond the Serpent's Maw Strait.

Patron Deities: Severin (Absolute Order) and Aethelis (Cosmic Harmony).

Role in World: Governed by an elite council of martial monks and arch-astrologers. Qing-Ling silk and enchanted jade talismans command fortune-tier prices in Calatara's markets.

F. The Freeholds of Caerwen

Culture & Aesthetics: Celtic / Agrarian woodland communes. Dense, primeval rainforests and rolling hills populated by halflings, Leshy, and spirit-fused Primal Folk.

Patron Deities: Vurrok (Primal Wilds) and Corvyna (Ancestral Tethers).

Role in World: The agricultural breadbasket of the central continent, supplying timber and grain while defending sacred grove borders with druidic wardens.

G. The Ashen Cradle of Mal-Kharum

Culture & Aesthetics: Volcanic badlands and forgotten monoliths. Active volcanoes, subterranean dark goblin strongholds, and ancient Titanborn carvings.

Patron Deities: Cankros (Rot & Rebirth) and Morwenna (Mourning).

Role in World: Epicenter of the ancient Resource Wars where the Void Primal was slain. A dangerous frontier for artifact prospectors and mercenary expeditions seeking lost Golden Age arcana.

6. Ancestries & Lineages Matrix

Core Lineages

Humans: Ambitious, adaptable, and spread across all major realms.

Elves: Long-lived custodians of Golden Age ruins, carrying solemn traditions tied to Morwenna.

Dwarves: Master stone-masons aligned with Brada, dwelling in deep mountain holds.

Gnomes: Eccentric scholars, astrologers, and chronomancers who venerate Aethelis.

Halflings: Close-knit, resilience-focused communities found throughout Cordoval farmlands and coastal ports.

The "Primal Folk" (Spirit-Fused Ancestries)

In ancient times, groups of Elves and Humans bonded with Primal Nature Spirits for survival, druidic evolution, or propagation.

Societal Integration: Today, "Folk" ancestries are fully integrated citizens (merchants, lords, knights, adventurers). While ancient prejudice occasionally resurfaces in fringe groups, they are generally recognized as standard mortals.

Lineages: Catfolk, Horsefolk, Bullfolk, Dogfolk, Foxfolk, Lizardfolk, Frogfolk, Snakefolk, Birdfolk, Monkeyfolk, Ratfolk, Hyenafolk (formerly Gnolls), Leshy, and Sprite.

Specialized Lineages

Shadowmen: Born during the dark ages following the Resource Wars when portals opened to the Primal Realm of Shadow. They appear as shadowy silhouettes reflecting mortal forms (human, elven, dwarven). They feel comfort in darkness but consider themselves fully mortal beings of the light world.

Titanborn: Large, ancient mountain-dwellers who hunt, gather, and carve monolithic art into cliff sides. They maintain a tense, respectful rivalry with Orcs and mountain Dwarves.

Goblins & Hobgoblins (Light vs. Dark Split):

Dark Goblins: Subterranean tribes that continue raiding surface settlements.

Light Goblins: Surface-dwelling tribes who rejected wild raiding generations ago to build towns and integrate with other ancestries.

Kobolds (Fey-Origin): Split historically into House, Ship, and Mine Kobolds. Modern Kobolds are expert tinkers, mariners, and miners who demand fair deals and equal respect.

7. Campaign Story Arc & 4-Act Progression

Prologue / Tutorial: The Siege of Calatara Citadel (Level 1)

Opening Cinematic: The campaign begins in media res inside the burning trade city of Calatara, under surprise attack by Torvallan vanguard forces led by Commander Vaelen.

The Savior Squad: The player's Level 1 custom avatar is trapped under falling debris and rescued by an elite squad of Cordovalen royal knights.

Gameplay Tutorial: The player temporarily controls these veteran high-level knights (Fighter, Wizard, Cleric, Rogue) during the escape. This showcases high-tier 3D elevation tactics, spell scaling, and action combinations before control shifts back to the Level 1 player avatar.

Act 1: Ashes and Opportunists (Levels 1–5)

Theme: Local crisis, political intrigue, and mercenary survival.

Main Quest: The avatar investigates the invasion. Cordoval recruits adventurers for a retaliatory campaign, while Torvallan emissaries offer secret contracts to justify their survival invasion.

Companion Recruitment: The avatar meets and recruits their core companion roster (e.g., a devoted Cordovalen paladin, a cynical Torvallan skirmisher, a Calataran free-trader).

The Rival Intro: The player repeatedly encounters Vaelen, Torvalla's brilliant field commander. Vaelen is not evil; he is fighting desperately to secure trade lines for his starving, ice-choked homeland.

Act 2: The Shattered Reliquaries & The Major Split (Levels 5–10)

Theme: Branching war paths, airship delves, and party conviction.

Main Quest & Critical Choice: Full war erupts. The player must choose a major campaign path:

Side with Cordoval: Defend the Grand Duchy, fight against Torvallan incursions, and battle Vaelen as a primary adversary.

Side with Torvalla & Party with Vaelen: Join Torvalla's campaign, directly party up with Vaelen as a temporary or permanent ally, and fight against Cordoval's oppressive trade blockade.

Independent Mercenary Path: Play both sides for profit, balancing delicate regional politics.

Companion Consequences: Choosing to ally with Torvalla or Cordoval directly affects companion approval. Strongly aligned companions may challenge the avatar's leadership or abandon the party.

Act 3: The Broken Balance & The Transformation (Levels 10–15)

Theme: Planar corruption, cosmic desperation, and tragic turning points.

Main Quest: A corrupt cult attempts to open the ancient planar scab left behind when the Void Primal was slain during the Resource Wars.

Vaelen's Transformation (Path Dependent): Desperate to save his people or turn the tide of the war, Vaelen accepts a dark bargain from the cult, unknowingly becoming the host vessel for the Slain Primal:

If Allied with Vaelen (Torvalla Path): Vaelen is in the player's group/faction. The player witnesses his tragic, agonizing transformation in person, as the Primal power consumes their ally from within during a desperate battle.

If Opposing Vaelen (Cordoval Path): Vaelen's desperate bargain and transformation happen off-screen or in antagonist encounters, revealing his terrifying new Primal-infused form on the battlefield.

Act 4: The Unraveling World (Levels 15–20)

Theme: Cosmic horror, planar battlefields, and ultimate choices.

Main Quest: Reality begins unraveling across the continent. The player must synthesize their alliances (uniting or subjugating the remaining forces of Cordoval, Torvalla, and Calatara) to assault the Planar Rift.

Climax: The player confronts Vaelen—now fully transformed into a tragic, world-ending vessel for the Slain Primal—and decides whether to redeem, destroy, or seal him away to determine the ultimate fate of the mortal realm.
